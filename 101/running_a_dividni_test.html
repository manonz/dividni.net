Running a Dividni individualised MCQ exam or test

Here's what's worked for us:

  • Run tests during normal class time in the normal class venue. This mitigates problems around "I have to work at that time", clashes with other tests, and students unable to find the venue.
  • Announce in advance that any approved test or exam aegrotats will be resolved by the student sitting a replacement test / exam. With Dividni, these are easy to supply!
    • Hint: If you have multiple streams, you might want have different "meta versions" of the test with a few questions swapped out against others covering different topic areas. This minimises cross-fertilisation from earlier to later streams.
  • We usually have the scripts inserted into sealed envelopes - this makes it easier to ensure that everyone starts at the same time.
  • Bring a few spare pencils, erasers and pencil sharpeners.
  • Prep the theatre before you let students in - look for cheat sheets "left behind".
  • Do ID check at the entrance(s). It's hard to do if you seat people in every seat and every row.
  • Have someone at / near the lectern to ensure students don't open the envelopes early or start writing early.
  • It's generally a good idea to separate students from their bags and phones - this minimises "ear bud issues" and the like. But: Make sure bags don't block fire exits!
  • Remind students to move towards the inside of each row of seats - people like their aisle seats but this isn't the time as you'll be having other students climb over them, which is a health and safety risk.
  • We usually give extra time (an extra 5 mins) to students if they're all seated five past the hour.
  • Tell students right at the start to rip the OMR sheet off. Ever so often, a student will damage theirs in doing so. In this case, they need a new script.
  • Tell students to enter their name and ID first - and then repeat this announcement a few times during the test. Tell them that they will lose one mark if they do not fill in the ID bubbles (fail to do so and you'll get to fill in the IDs for a few based on their name!).
  • For tests under an hour, tell students in advance that you will not allow toilet breaks without a medical certificate. We've seen multiple cases of students taking photos of their tests and the smuggling these out during toilet breaks to have them solved via a helper with ChatGPT (and earbud transmission back into the room).
  • Run specials (students who are given extra time due to their circumstances) with maybe 30 minutes offset. This allows all course staff to be present at the start of the main test (when the workload is highest). One of them can then leave after the start of the test and head to the specials venue.
  • At the end of the test, collect the OMR sheets BEFORE you allow students to leave.
