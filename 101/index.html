DIVIDNI 101

This course is intended for teaching staff wanting to use the Dividni UI system to:

  • Author simple individualised multiple-choice questions ("truth" and "XYZ" questions)
  • Create individualised paper test and exam scripts in an arbitrary number of versions from these questions using optical mark recognition (OMR) sheets 
  • Scan and grade such tests and exams 
  • Manage questions across multiple assessments

Note: Dividni UI is currently limited to the University of Auckland. Please use the command-line version if you are not from the University of Auckland.

Head for the Modules page for everything from an introduction to an FAQ. 

Why do I want to use Dividni?

How much time do you spend each semester on:

  • Making up questions for your mid-semester test and exam because last year's are now in the public domain?
  • Organising venues for your mid-semester test?
  • Finding colleagues and tutors / markers to help you invigilate them?
  • Marking tests and exams?
  • Dealing with replacement grade calculations for students who have missed tests or exams and have been awarded an aegrotat, all the while suspecting that some aegrotats may not be genuine and that the student may be attempting to pass on the strength of assignments that are not their own work?

If your answer is "more than I'd like", Dividni is for you.

Dividni lets you:

  • Make up questions and entire tests / exams quickly.
  • Author questions so they can turn into a myriad of quite different instances, making questions highly reusable.
  • Author questions that defy memorisation and require your students to calculate, analyse and evaluate.
  • Run your assessments in class and where needed time-shifted for specials. No need for extra invigilation workforce. 
  • Mark tests and exams for large classes in a couple of hours rather than a few days.
  • Provide make-up tests and exams for students who have missed the original.

Dividni assessments are written, pen on paper, yet leverage the power of digital.
