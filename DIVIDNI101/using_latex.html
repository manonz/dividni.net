Using LaTeX for mathematical formulas in Dividni MCQ questions

If you subject uses formulas (e.g., maths, statistics, physics, engineering, ...) you may wish to use formulas in the questions or appendices of your test or exam. This is easy as long as you work in HTML mode (where your markup is visible in the question editor) or in the code editor. There are three options for inserting formulas:

  1. You can enclose simple inline expressions in single dollar signs, such as $\sin(x)$, which gives you \(\sin(x)\) in your text.
  2. The same effect results from using \( and \) as in \(\sin(x)\), also giving you \(\sin(x)\) in your text.
  3. Enclosing expressions in double dollar signs, such as $$\gamma=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}}$$, renders them as equations offset from the surrounding text: $$\gamma=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}}$$

For rendering, you can choose between MathJax and KaTeX in the Exam Setup dialog. Both are hosted locally and do not pull the latest libraries from the web. Read: If you need recent features, you may be out of luck or need to talk to us.
