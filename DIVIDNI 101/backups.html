You now have valuable data. Protect it: Backing up your questions and exam settings. Or share it.

We have built the Dividni UI system with robustness in mind:

  • Each of you has their own dedicated database - low risk of anyone else's misadventures on the platform damaging your data.
  • The server is a virtual machine operated by Connect and shouldn't go up in smoke.
  • Everything's encrypted our own way and even a breach of the university's login system shouldn't expose your exam data.

But even so - our systems are part of a complex IT ecosystem. Not everything is under our control, and we're not perfect, either.

We're fairly confident that, even if the Web MCQ Generator UI or the scanning system should fail, we'd be able to support you. The one thing we can't help you with is re-write your questions and re-arrange your scripts.

So we'd like you to plan for the worst: A complete loss of the Dividni online systems.

Your Plan B is a regular backup of your question banks and exams. This is how:

  • You can back up any question banks by clicking on the "Manage" button to the right of the question bank and selecting "Export". This will download a ZIP file with all questions in the question bank to your local computer. You can use this file to re-upload the questions it contains later. If all falls to pieces and Connect doesn't get the web system back up again, we can also use these in the command line version of Dividni.
  • Likewise, the same works for any document in the Document Bank: Click on "Manage" and then on "Export" to download the document to your computer.
  • This also works for images in the Image Bank.
  • In a test / exam preview view, hit "Save" (on top of the preview). Then click on the down arrow on the right side of the same Save button. This opens a menu that will let you download (export) the full exam, the questions only (C# files) or just the question order.

Sharing your questions and documents

A backup of your question banks and documents is also a convenient way of sharing questions that you have authored with colleagues. Remember: ZIP files with questions can simply be uploaded into any of their question banks or exams.
