Practicalities: Getting a Dividni MCQ Exam Generator Web UI account

THIS PAGE IS FOR UNIVERSITY OF AUCKLAND STAFF ONLY

To get an account to access the Dividni MCQ Exam Generator Web UI, please contact Ulrich Speidel u.speidel@auckland.ac.nz.

We'll give you a login and password. This is different from your University of Auckland SSO login and may be shared among a teaching team.

To access the Dividni MCQ Exam Generator Web UI, head to:

https://www.dividni.auckland.ac.nz

Note: You will need to be University of Auckland staff and either be on the university network or the university VPN (if away from campus).

Your account will come pre-populated with a question bank for DIVIDNI 101 and a sample test / exam and the associated documents.

Rather than modifying the question bank and sample test / exam and documents for your own test / exam, we suggest strongly that you copy the cover (prologue), answer sheet and epilogue documents you want to use (via the "Manage" button next to them) and create a new question bank and test / exam to work with.

Not University of Auckland staff?

Contact us to see whether we may be able to help you.
